# Math Tug of War 3D — visible rope grip: owner review pack

Branch `hotfix/math-tug-of-war-3d-visible-rope-grip`, cut from the production commit `9c3917d`
(v1.2.22, `dpl_Ejn4Qooo96nAfvkdmsAabUtZ84QH`). Captured by
`node scripts/capture-math-tug-of-war-3d-grip-review.mjs --before <checkout of 9c3917d>`: the shipped game
document under its production route CSP, real Teams matches played through the keypads, headless Chromium
with the machine's GPU. "Before" frames come from the production commit's runtime (byte-identical to what
mathnexa.com serves: all 59 runtime files compared on 2026-10-14).

## Why the v1.2.22 grip did not show in the game

1. **The rope ran behind the bodies.** The pullers stood 0.16 m *in front of* the rope line, towards the
   camera, so every fist was on the far side of the torso. From the gameplay camera most hands were hidden
   (0 px) and the rest were a few pixels at the belly, folded behind the hip (`01`, `02`).
2. **The grips hugged the hips** (front +0.10 m, back −0.05 m from the hips), so the forearms folded across
   the stomach instead of reaching for the rope.
3. **The fist was a fixed curl** (0.6) placed by two hand-tuned offsets; close-ups showed open, spread
   fingers lying on the rope.
4. **The rope tube was wound inside-out** (all 5,280 triangles faced inwards, also in production): its near
   wall was culled, so anything pressed into the rope showed through it as a hole.
5. **The tests could not see any of this.** The "rope through the fist" check measured the distance from the
   computed fist point to a rope that is *built through that same point* (always ≈ 0); `reachMiss` measures
   arm reach, not visibility; the review close-ups were taken by a debug camera placed outside the body,
   which sees round the torso. Nothing measured what the player's camera shows.

Not the cause (checked): stale assets or CDN caching (the document versions every entry URL with the
runtime content hash, and production serves byte-identical files), model versions, tiers (the grip code is
the same on every tier; classic is the SVG scene), the 3D toggle (on by default on capable devices).

## What changed

- The rope now runs on the **camera side** of every body (pullers 0.22 m behind it, rope at belt height
  0.87 m); the body stance itself is unchanged.
- **Fists solved from each character's own hand skin** (`scene3d/grip.js`, data in `scene3d/fist-data.js`,
  generated by `scripts/math-tug-of-war-3d/solve-fists.mjs`): the rope crosses the palm diagonally as in a
  power grip, every finger closes until its skin touches the rope, the thumb closes over it. Every one of the
  twelve hands closes every digit and covers 71–83 % of the rope's circumference.
- Each frame the fist is laid along the rope and the arm solved to it; the rope is threaded through each fist
  along the fist's own channel; the near elbow stays low and turns so the forearm crosses the rope; taking
  hold, letting go and the end-of-match change of pose all blend (no snapping); the brow wipe eases.
- The rope tube is wound outwards, smoothly sampled (no overshoot at the fists), 12-sided.
- New measurement shared by the tests, the hosted review and this pack:
  `e2e/math-tug-of-war/player-camera-hands.ts` renders the scene from the **player's camera** with each
  puller's hand skin in a flat colour (bodies still hide what they hide) and counts how much of each hand is
  visible and how much of it touches the visible rope.
