## Before each post

Use the checklist at the end of `plan.md`, in particular:

1. Check https://mathnexa.com/pricing the same day. If it no longer says "24-hour free trial, then
   $5.99 USD a month", do not post: the images and captions need a new render.
2. Open the post's landing URL (with its UTM tail) and confirm the page loads.
3. Read the caption once more: spelling, price wording, the link, 3 to 5 hashtags.
4. Paste the alt text into the platform's alt text field when posting. If the field is short, the first
   sentence ("MathNexa advert: ...") works on its own.
5. Post from your own accounts. Nothing was connected, uploaded or scheduled for you.

## For the owner to decide

1. **Homework artwork.** The homework image is the approved s2 artwork with its small "ANSWER KEY READY"
   tag covered by a patch of the header's own flat colour (rgb 12, 60, 87), so the Homework images make
   no answer-key claim. Nothing else in the artwork was changed. If any edit to the artwork is unwanted,
   the alternative is a different homework picture.
2. **Games headline.** The games image names both games ("Math Bingo Live and Math Race Champions")
   instead of a general "math games" headline, because /math-games also lists games that need a
   subscription. The badge reads exactly "Free with a MathNexa account".
3. **"Cancel any time"** is on the three 4:5 images and in most captions (/pricing: "Cancel any time from
   Subscription in your MathNexa account"). The squares leave it out for space. Remove it if preferred.
4. **"y = 2.5x" in the Online Math Prep artwork.** In the approved s3 artwork, unchanged here, the graph
   line runs across the "=" of "y = 2.5x". Zoomed in, it can look like "≠". The label is small at phone
   size, but say if you want a different picture.
5. **Landing pages.** The images link to each product's own page (/homework-pdfs, /quiz-pdfs, /math-games).
   The PDF videos in `plan.md` link to /online-math-prep instead. Both return 200 and show the same offer.
