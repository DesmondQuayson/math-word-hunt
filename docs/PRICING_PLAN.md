# Pricing Plan

Recommended beta pricing, subject to teacher interviews and purchasing tests:

| Plan                        | Introductory price | Intended buyer             |
| --------------------------- | -----------------: | -------------------------- |
| Free                        |                 $0 | Any classroom              |
| Teacher Pro annual          |           $39/year | Individual teacher         |
| Teacher Pro standard annual |           $59/year | Individual teacher         |
| Small school, 10 teachers   |          $299/year | Department or small school |
| School site license         |          $699/year | One school building        |

The introductory individual price should be offered to beta schools for their
first year. Do not finalize pricing until at least 10 teachers have completed a
real classroom trial and at least five purchasing conversations have tested
budget and procurement constraints.

Commercial launch also requires a license system, purchase/renewal flow, tax
handling, refund policy, support contact, and legal entity. None is implemented
in this repository.
