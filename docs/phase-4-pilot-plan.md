# Controlled teacher pilot

Pilot 3–10 adult teachers for 2–4 weeks. Eligibility: owner-invited educators who accept the preview/privacy notice and can avoid student personal data. Onboarding covers preview labeling, account verification/recovery, Free/Pro test packaging, canonical classroom launch, issue reporting, and immediate stop instructions.

Supported: teacher accounts, profile, class/activity drafts within limits, canonical v7 play, aggregate prototype boundaries, and Stripe test-mode demonstrations if later enabled. Unsupported: real payment, students/rosters, assignments, managed sessions, persisted reports, AI, district administration, permanent deletion, and production reliability. Rules: no student names/emails/work, no sensitive school data, no passwords/tokens/payment details in reports, and no attempt to bypass access controls.

Use an owner-controlled support form asking for workflow, browser/device, approximate time, impact, accessibility/Smart Board observations, and reproducible steps. Do not collect page dumps or personal data by default. Classify issues using SEV-1–4 in the incident runbook.

Success: invited teachers can verify/recover accounts, create permitted records, launch and complete the game on target devices, understand limits/preview status, and report no cross-account/security or blocking accessibility failures. Exit at the planned date or sooner for security/privacy incident, real-payment risk, recurring data loss, inaccessible core workflow, or unacceptable support load. Rollback blocks access and resets disposable preview data. At exit, export minimized feedback, honor deletion requests under the approved interim process, and reset/dispose pilot data.

Feedback: Was setup understandable? Could you plan and launch without help? Were limits honest? What failed on keyboard, mobile, projector, or Smart Board? Was math content clear and age-appropriate? What would block classroom use? Pilot billing is test mode only and creates no charge.
