# Phase 6 privacy and acceptable-use draft

Status: draft for owner and appropriate legal/privacy review. It is not legal
advice, final policy, compliance certification, or district approval.

## Restricted Preview

Math Vocabulary Hunt Pilot Preview is a restricted test environment for
approved adult teachers. It is not a public Production service. Features may
change, and unsupported features must not be treated as available.

## Permitted use

Participants may evaluate teacher authentication, teacher-only planning tools,
the canonical vocabulary game, accessibility, support instructions, and the
non-persistent feedback template. Use only teacher-recognizable labels that do
not identify students.

## Prohibited use

Do not involve students or enter student names, emails, IDs, rosters, work,
grades, IEP information, behavioral records, or other sensitive school data.
Do not enter passwords, tokens, cookies, provider secrets, payment details, or
raw authentication payloads into planning or feedback fields. Do not attempt
cross-account access, public sharing, payment, or Production use.

## Data use and access

The Preview limits collection to the adult-teacher fields and sanitized events
listed in [`phase-6-data-inventory.md`](phase-6-data-inventory.md). Identity,
RLS, ownership, account status, and server authorization control access. There
is no analytics provider, session replay, third-party feedback persistence, or
live billing in Phase 6.

## Restriction, exit, and deletion

A participant may log out and stop participation at any time. Contact the
pilot coordinator using the channel through which pilot access was provided to
request immediate account restriction or staged deletion review. A deletion
request restricts new protected activity; permanent deletion is disabled until
the owner approves policy and execution separately.

## Incidents and unsupported features

Report suspected student-data entry, cross-account exposure, secret exposure,
or public access immediately through the access-provisioning channel. Do not
include prohibited data in the report. Managed sessions, persisted reports,
analytics, billing, payment, organization licensing, SSO, and Production are
not supported. No charge will be collected during the proposed pilot.
