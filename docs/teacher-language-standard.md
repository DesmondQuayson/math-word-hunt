# Teacher Language Standard

Status: Phase 1C.5C frontend language contract.

## Vocabulary

Use these terms consistently:

- **Class**: a teacher-owned grouping of activities and aggregate history; not
  a student directory.
- **Activity**: curriculum and classroom play settings; not an assignment sent
  to student accounts.
- **Live session**: the future teacher-managed setup and recovery concept.
- **Current v7 game**: the real, independently playable classroom game.
- **Demonstration data**: generic, server-enabled examples that are not saved.
- **Aggregate report**: class, activity, lesson, vocabulary, team, or session
  summaries without individual student records.
- **Curriculum status**: Ready, Thin—Combine Mode recommended, Coming soon, or
  Teacher review pending.

## Voice and action rules

- Start with what a teacher recognizes and controls: “Review activity setup,”
  not “open authoring prototype.”
- Name the result: “Open current v7 game,” not “launch path.”
- Say what is unavailable in plain language: “Saving is unavailable,” not
  “persistence is not implemented.”
- Keep current and future capabilities in separate cards or paragraphs.
- Put “Demonstration data” before every fixture region.
- Explain empty states with the next safe action.
- Use “student-level tracking” rather than technical analytics terminology.
- Do not describe a checked definition as publisher-approved or expert-reviewed.

Avoid backend terms in teacher-facing copy, including persistence, canonical
source, adapter, entitlement, fixture, realtime service, production-default,
and trusted backend. Those terms remain appropriate in engineering documents.

This is functional product language, not final marketing copy. Do not add
unsupported impact, engagement, mastery, alignment, or learning claims.
