# Game Rules

Each team receives the same puzzle and a fresh timer.

1. Begin at a visible endpoint, corner, or junction.
2. Keep one finger, stylus, or mouse button pressed in Trace Mode.
3. Trace every required edge exactly once.
4. Revisit a junction only when the route requires it.
5. Never reuse a completed line.
6. Never cross a completed line outside a registered junction.
7. Stay in the target corridor; do not jump through blank space.
8. A wrong route may reach a genuine dead end.
9. A correct Euler trail completes the puzzle.

Hidden Start Challenge puzzles are connected Euler trails with exactly two
odd-degree nodes. The interface does not reveal those endpoints until the
second hint.

If one team solves, it earns the round point. If both solve, the faster team
earns the point. Times within 0.10 seconds are a tie and both teams score,
unless only one team used a hint; in that dead heat, the no-hint team wins.

Exact failure titles are **STAY ON THE SHAPE**, **KEEP YOUR FINGER DOWN**,
**LINE ALREADY USED**, **LINES CROSSED**, and **DEAD END**.

Restart Attempt clears the trace but preserves the running clock. Restart Turn
returns to the ready screen and restores the full turn time.
