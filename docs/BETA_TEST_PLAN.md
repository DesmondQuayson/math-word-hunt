# Teacher Beta Test Plan

## Cohort

- 10–15 teachers
- Grades 3–8, with at least three different subject areas
- Mix of projector/mouse, touch display, and Smart Board classrooms
- At least two accessibility-focused sessions using Node Mode

## Session

1. Teacher opens the public route without coaching.
2. Teacher begins Quick Play.
3. Class completes one Easy, one Medium, and one Hard round.
4. Teacher runs a custom Classroom Match.
5. At least three teachers run a 4+ team tournament.
6. Teacher closes and reopens the installed app offline.

## Measures

- Time from landing page to first Ready screen: target under 60 seconds
- Successful first trace without adult correction: target 80%
- Accidental lift/off-path rate by device type
- Round disputes or perceived unfairness: target zero
- Teacher-rated readability at the back of the room: target 4/5
- Would-use-again and willingness-to-pay interviews
- Crash, frozen-input, blank-offline-start, and state-loss incidents

## Exit gate

Commercial launch requires no open P0/P1 issue, no fairness dispute caused by
the engine, successful offline restart at all beta sites, and at least 8 of 10
teachers saying they would run another match. Record findings separately from
marketing claims.
